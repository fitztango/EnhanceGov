The Pharos Method™

Two Layers. One Diagnostic Truth.

Most assessments only measure what an organization says it does. Pharos measures the delta between declared intent and operational reality — a gap that, unaddressed, becomes your biggest strategic liability.

  • Business Layer — Governance posture, technology declarations, compliance frameworks, strategic documentation
  • Human Layer — Cultural readiness, change adoption, behavioral signals, informal decision patterns
  • Divergence Score — A proprietary index quantifying the gap across 12 dimensions
  • Financial Intelligence — TCO modeling with Cloud, On-Premises, Hybrid, Shadow Cost analysis
  • Executive Report — Board-ready PDF with heatmap and prioritized recommendations
