Strategic Advisory Services

From diagnostic assessments to long-term strategic engagements, EnhanceGov brings intelligence-community rigor to organizational challenges across four primary service lines.

Service Line 01

Pharos Gap Diagnostics™

Our flagship diagnostic platform measures the gap between your organization's declared posture and its operational reality across 12 dimensions — governance, technology, culture, change readiness, and more.

Delivered as both a self-service platform and a facilitated advisory engagement, Pharos provides executives with the clarity to make decisions based on organizational reality — not aspiration.

  • 12-dimension Business & Human Layer scoring
  • Divergence Heatmap with segment routing
  • Financial Intelligence / TCO modeling
  • Board-ready executive PDF reports
  • Benchmark mode with Gartner/IDC/Flexera data

Service Line 02

Executive Strategic Advisory

Long-form advisory engagements for C-suite and senior leadership teams navigating technology transformation, governance restructuring, or organizational change management.

Drawing on intelligence tradecraft's model of structured analytical support to decision-makers, our advisory engagements provide a sustained intelligence function — surfacing risk, validating assumptions, and briefing leadership on organizational reality.

  • Retained strategic advisory (monthly/quarterly cadence)
  • Technology governance framework development
  • AI readiness and implementation advisory
  • Board briefings and investor-facing diagnostics

"The best intelligence brief doesn't just tell you what happened. It tells you what it means for your next decision."

Service Line 03

Organizational Intelligence Audits

A structured, time-bound engagement that applies the Pharos diagnostic methodology to a specific organizational challenge — a merger integration, a technology implementation failure, a governance gap surfaced by a compliance finding.

Unlike broad consulting frameworks, our audits follow the intelligence community's structured analytic techniques: hypothesis generation, evidence collection, source validation, and confidence-weighted findings.

  • Merger & acquisition readiness assessment
  • Post-implementation reviews
  • Compliance gap forensics
  • Technology adoption failure analysis

Engagement Structure

Scoping

Define the analytical question and scope of evidence collection.

Collection

Structured intake across document review, interviews, and system analysis.

Analysis

Confidence-weighted findings with source attribution and gap mapping.

Briefing

Executive brief with prioritized recommendations and risk ratings.
