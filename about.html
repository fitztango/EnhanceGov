The Pharos Method™

The Gap Between Policy and Reality Is Where Risk Lives

Most organizations invest heavily in governance frameworks, technology roadmaps, and strategic plans. But the documents rarely tell the whole story. What employees actually do — how decisions get made, where data flows, what processes get skipped — is often invisible to leadership.

The Pharos Method™ borrows from intelligence tradecraft: collect, validate, and analyze signals across both the Business Layer (declared posture) and the Human Layer (operational reality) to surface the divergence that creates organizational risk.
