EnhanceGov LLC was founded on a conviction: that the analytical rigor required in the intelligence community — where getting it wrong costs lives — should be the standard for organizational decision-making.

The Pharos Method™ Origin

The CIA Had One Rule: Truth Is Non-Negotiable

In the intelligence community, analysis lives or dies on the gap between what sources claim and what evidence supports. Analysts are trained to surface that gap, name it, and account for it — regardless of what leadership wants to hear.

In the corporate world, that gap is rarely surfaced at all. Governance documents declare one reality. Culture enacts another. Technology roadmaps describe aspirations. Operations reflect constraints. And the executives who need to make decisions are systematically insulated from the divergence.

EnhanceGov applies the CIA's analytical framework — collect all signals, validate sources, surface the delta, brief the principal — to organizational consulting. The result is the Pharos Method™: a structured diagnostic methodology that illuminates what your organization's own documents can't show you.

Leadership

Stephanie Fitzpatrick

Founder & Principal — EnhanceGov LLC

Stephanie Fitzpatrick is a former CIA intelligence analyst turned strategic advisor, whose career has been defined by one discipline: finding the truth organizations can't see from the inside.

After serving in the intelligence community, Stephanie recognized that the same analytical breakdowns that cause intelligence failures — confirmation bias, institutional blind spots, the gap between declared and operational reality — are endemic to organizational decision-making at every level.

She founded EnhanceGov LLC to bring that discipline to organizations across healthcare, federal and local government, energy, and SaaS — building proprietary frameworks that translate intelligence tradecraft into organizational intelligence.
